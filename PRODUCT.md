# WebScope

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Security analysts, application-security engineers, and authorized penetration
testers conducting local, evidence-driven web application assessments.

## Product Purpose
Connect authorized discovery, deterministic observations, source evidence,
analyst conclusions, risk decisions, and reproducible technical reports.

## Positioning
An investigation workstation, not an exploit framework or an AI scanner.
Every finding has inspectable source evidence and versioned rule provenance.

## Operating Context
Local-first desktop investigation with responsive access. The analyst defines
authorization and scope before any network collection, then reviews evidence
before drawing conclusions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Python/FastAPI/SQLite backend, React/TypeScript frontend. No paid service,
external intelligence, or language model is required. Active checks are
explicit, bounded, read-only, and scoped. Target content is always untrusted.

## Brand Commitments
The supplied brief fixes a dark, restrained, dense-but-readable technical
analyst workstation. No marketing filler, synthetic counters, or fake actions.

## Evidence on Hand
The full supplied specification and deterministic loopback fixtures. No
third-party assessment results or real-world interoperability claims.

## Product Principles
Evidence first; authorization first; passive analysis before safe active checks;
automated observations separate from analyst conclusions; transparent risk.

## Hosted Workspaces
User-directed extension: Vercel frontend and Render API, **no sign-in**.
Anonymous browser sessions contain synthetic examples and temporary uploads.
Session-owned uploads/results expire after 24 hours, can be deleted immediately
with End session, and are cleared earlier by hosted restart/redeploy.
Prominent retention and export reminders are part of the product, not optional
legal fine print. Tabs in the same browser profile share a session.
Hosted deployments are import-only; authorized target collection remains local.
